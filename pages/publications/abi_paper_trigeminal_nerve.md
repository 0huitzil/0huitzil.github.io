### Summary:

The trigeminal nerve receives its name from the three nerves that comprise it: 
ophthalmic, maxillary and mandibular (cyan, magenta and orange, respectively, in the picture).
Fun fact: both the trigeminal nerve and the [spinal nerve]({{ "/publications/abi_paper_wbs/" | relative_url }})
 scaffolds are actually very distorted tube networks! 

This scaffold was created as part of the [SPARC](https://sparc.science/) project, and it is parametrised according to anatomical data, ready to be fit to experimental datasets!
