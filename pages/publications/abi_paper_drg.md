## Generic human dorsal root ganglion scaffold

The dorsal root ganglion (aka the **DRG**) is a cluster of neurons situated on the dorsal root of the 
spinal nerve. These ganglia contain, among other things, the cell bodies of primary sensory neurons. 
The picture of our scaffold shown above actually contains a whole spinal nerve, and the DRG is the bulbous part on the upper branch. 

This scaffold was created as a building block for the [spinal nerves]({{ "/publications/abi_paper_trigeminal_nerve/" | relative_url }}) scaffold, 
which itself is embedded into the [3D whole body]({{ "/publications/abi_paper_wbs/" | relative_url }}), as part of the [SPARC](https://sparc.science/) project. The scaffold is parametrised according to anatomical data, ready to be fit to experimental datasets!
