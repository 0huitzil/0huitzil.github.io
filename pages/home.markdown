# About me

## Career

I am a **mathematical modeller** and software developer. Currently working as software 
developer and mathematical modeller at the 
Auckland Bioengineering Institute ([ABI]({{ "/current_work/" | relative_url }})) creating
computational models of anatomical structures since 2025. 


I love applying my mathematical knowledge to the life sciences,
from physiology to anatomy and epidemiology. A passion which I obtained while doing my
Bachelor's Degree in Applied Mathematics at the
Instituto Técnologico Autónomo de México. (ITAM) (2015-2021). I particularly enjoy 
looking at these systems through the lenses of dynamical systems, thanks in part to my 
undergrad supervisor [Victor Francisco Breña Medina](http://lya.fciencias.unam.mx/victorbrena/).

The next step of my journey took me all the way to the other side of the world to 
[Waipapa Taumata Rau | The University of Auckland](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en.html) in New Zealand
where I had the opportunity to study the mathematical modelling of Ca2+ oscillations in cells
under the tutelage of [Vivien Kirk](https://profiles.auckland.ac.nz/v-kirk) 
and [James Sneyd](https://profiles.auckland.ac.nz/jsne010). Which resulted in a series of three
publications which you can ready more about [here]({{ "/publications/" | relative_url }}).

I have always enjoyed mathematics, but I am a firm believer that they excel most when taken
out into the field and used to help questions beyond the abstract realm. So far I have had 
the fortune of working in **chemistry**, **physiology** and (most recently) **anatomy**, and I hope
that in the future I can expand my skill set to encompass many other fields. 

## Personal life

Aside from mathematics, my passion is history in all its varieties and flavours. 
I believe that understanding the past and the lives of those that came before us
can only enrich our current understanding of the world. And at the same time give us a glimpse 
into completely different ways of living and experiencing the world. When it comes to 
modelling, the context is intrinsically tied to the question one is trying to answer. 

I believe in the power of local community organisations, not only as a provider or 
services and resources, but also as a way to connect with the place one lives in. I spend 
some of my spare time volunteering for the 
[Auckland Library of Tools](https://www.aucklandlibraryoftools.com/). It is an 
incredible organisation with a very noble goal! I also volunteer for the 
[Auckland Zoo](https://www.aucklandzoo.co.nz/) as a visitor assistant, doing a little bit
to aid their conservation efforts.
