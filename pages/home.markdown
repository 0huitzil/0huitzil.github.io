# About me

## Career

I am a **mathematical modeller** and software developer. I have always enjoyed mathematics, 
but I am a firm believer that they excel most when taken
out into the field and used to help questions beyond the abstract realm. So far I have had 
the fortune of working in **chemistry**, **physiology**, **immunology** 
and (most recently) **anatomy**, and I hope that in the future I can expand my 
skill set to encompass many other fields. 

In particular, I enjoy looking at these systems from a dynmical systems angle, 
a fascination I gained from working with my supervisor undergrad supervisor 
[Victor Francisco Breña Medina](http://lya.fciencias.unam.mx/victorbrena/) while doing my
Bachelor's Degree in Applied Mathematics at the
Instituto Técnologico Autónomo de México. (ITAM) (2015-2021)

The next step of my journey took me all the way to the other side of the world to 
[Waipapa Taumata Rau | The University of Auckland](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en.html) in New Zealand
where I had the opportunity to study the mathematical modelling of Ca2+ oscillations in cells
under the tutelage of [Vivien Kirk](https://profiles.auckland.ac.nz/v-kirk) 
and [James Sneyd](https://profiles.auckland.ac.nz/jsne010) (2021–2025). This resulted in a series of 
[three publications]({{ "/publications/" | relative_url }}) which describe the progression of a model:
 from the initial data from the experimental 
group to the modelling itself and some of the more theoretical mathematical results we can obtain 
from analysing these systems.

Since 2025, I am working as a software developer and mathematical modeller at the 
Auckland Bioengineering Institute ([ABI]({{ "/current_work/" | relative_url }})) creating
computational models of anatomical structures. These structures, which we call **scaffolds** are
used in a range of healthcare-related projects. With them, my team and I create 
interactive visualization tools, mapping interfaces and even statistical analysis of population-level data
